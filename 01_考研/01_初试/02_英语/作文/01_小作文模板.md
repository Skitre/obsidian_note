## 段前
- 书信（称呼语）
	- Dear....../To who it may concern, 后者更官方
	- 知道的后跟姓、名、职位
	- 不知道的跟 Sir or Madam or friend (s)
## 首段
**次要目的（寒暄）+主要目的（作文主题）**

**寒暄**
Thank you for your last letter regarding.../...
Congradulate you for...
Apologize for...
I hope this letter finds you well.
Konwing that...

I'm......../AS........ (通常用于公函，用于自我介绍，私人信件一般不用)
**主要目的**
I am writing this letter in order to...
- 感谢 express my heartfelt gratitude/ appreciate for your unwavering support and immense help...
- 道歉 clarify some misunderstandings for... / offer my sincerest apologize for my...
- 邀请 to extend my cordial invitation to you for
- 通知 inform you that…/ notify you of the meeting
- 建议 offer you some practical suggestions/recommendations/improvements about
- 投诉 express my deep dissatisfaction with
- 表扬 praise/ commend your excellent customer service
- 咨询 inquire about
- 推荐 recommend … to you.
- 求助 request your assistance
- 帮助 offer my support.
- 求职 apply for a position as an assistant
- 辞职 request to resign from my present position
- 计划 describe my recent plan/ provide further details about the plan 
- 安排 specify the event arrangement. / outline my daily schedule.
- 确认 confirm our appointment.
- 总结 summarize the main points of the meeting.
- 强调 highlight the importance of this matter.

### 主题内容
**此处内容撰写策略分为上中下三策**
上策为：自由发挥，写明关键的原因/结果/建议/内容
中策为：选用一会所写的合适的公式
下册为：To begin with... Then... And... 强行用连接词叙述
**以下为可供选择的例句**
- 是什么（**举办地点，集合地点，介绍地点**）
	- It's located/It's will be held in/beside 地点
	- We will gather in the 地点 and set off to the spot, (please make sure you are available then)
	- 主语 will be there to provide you with necessary guidance/assistance
	- It's high recommended to be properly/fully dressed/prepared.
- 为什么（**由小到大**）
	- 主语 have an official award/recognition/qualification from the authority
	- 主语 be awarded with/won the Intangible/material Cultural Heritage Award issued by the United Nations
	- 主语 enjoys a widespread popularity/reputation/concern among/on multiple social platforms
	- As for me, ..... (亲身体会)
- 怎么做
	- It's advisable to put things into perspective and establish correct values about 某事/某物 primarily.
	- You could look relevant advance/solution/info in this aspect on ZhiHu or other community
	- A practical and reasonable plan/scheme should be promptly carried out and remain high on agenda.
**注意，以上三部分无关顺序，也不是必须全部用上，根据题材来定，比如说建议信（怎么办+为什么），通知（是什么+为什么（+怎么做））**
